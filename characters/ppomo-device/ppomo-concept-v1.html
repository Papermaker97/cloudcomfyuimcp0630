Still open in v1

  • Wire between butt sensor and head electronics: candidates are silicone-insulated stranded wire or Qwiic. None tested for repeated flexing.
  • Haptic: DRV2605L is a candidate driver. No motor chosen or tested.
  • Count storage: onboard flash vs. microSD, and whether an RTC is needed at all.
Layout follows Tom Igoe's earlier feedback: fragile electronics in the head, which is not squeezed; the sensor in the butt; the connecting wire settled by experiment. Counts here are simulated by the buttons on this page. Squeezes less than 300 ms apart are ignored, to show one squeeze = one event.
